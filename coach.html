What is "Fico"?

The perfect way to approach well-being.

Fico (noun)

/ˈfiːkoʊ/

1. Botany: The Italian word for fig, a soft, nutrient-rich fruit known for its delicate skin, complex texture and natural sweetness.

2. Lifestyle: A state of feeling balanced, confident, and at ease with oneself, where physical well-being, energy and lifestyle are in harmony.

3. By extension: An approach to fitness and wellness that focuses on finding one’s own rhythm, building sustainable habits and feeling good without pressure or extremes.

Fico is built on that idea.

Today, most fitness programs are rigid, intense, and hard to maintain. They don’t adapt to your lifestyle, your energy or your rhythm.

Fico offers a different approach to fitness and wellness.

It helps you:
• Find your own rhythm,
• Build a routine that fits your lifestyle,
• Improve your well-being without pressure.

This is not about extreme performance, it’s about creating a sustainable fitness and wellness routine that works for you.

A balanced approach, inspired by the fig.

The fig is a complex and rich fruit, but also delicate.

To fully develop, it depends on three essential elements:

• The leaf, which sustains and captures energy over time.
• The flesh, which stores and transforms nutrients,
• The skin, which shapes, protects, and defines.

Your body works the same way.

Building strength, losing fat or improving your fitness is never about one action alone. It’s about focusing on the right element, at the right moment.

That’s where Fico comes in.

Fico as fitness

Training adapted to your lifestyle

Three programs. Three focuses.

Fico is designed for people who want a personalized fitness program that fits into real life.

BODY RECOMPOSITION

Build sustainability.

This program is about long-term transformation.

Instead of focusing only on short-term results, it helps your body adapt, evolve, and stay consistent over time. You build strength, improve body composition, and create habits that last.

Like the leaf, which continues to support life beyond the fruit itself, this approach is about durability: keeping your progress over time.

Ideal if you want a sustainable fitness program that improves your body without extreme phases.

The body you want. Built to last.

WEIGHT LOSS

Rebalance from within.

Weight loss is not just about losing weight: it’s about changing how your body stores and uses energy.

This program focuses on reducing body fat while maintaining energy and muscle. Through controlled nutrition, efficient training, and consistency, your body learns to function in a more balanced way.

Like the flesh of the fig, this is where transformation happens, where reserves are used, adjusted, and optimized.

Ideal if your goal is a healthy weight loss program that feels controlled, not restrictive.

Real change starts from within.

MASS GAINING

Build structure and presence.

Gaining muscle is about more than size: it’s about structure.

This program helps you develop strength, improve physical presence, and increase your capacity to handle effort. With progressive training and proper nutrition, your body becomes stronger and more resilient.

Like the skin of the fig, this is what gives shape and definition. It protects, supports, and creates the visible structure of the body.

Ideal if you want a muscle gain program that builds both strength and confidence.

Your body reflects what you build.

One body. One balance.
These programs are not isolated paths. Depending on your goal, we focus on one area, but your body always works as a whole.
